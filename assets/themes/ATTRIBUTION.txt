ORIGINAL PALETTE ATTRIBUTION

Original palettes belong to their respective authors. These Tagcast JSON
adaptations map the palettes to Tagcast's existing colour roles and include
small contrast adjustments; they are not official ports or endorsements.

Amber Midnight · Tagcast
https://github.com/cyberdeliaAI/tagcast/tree/main/docs/themes#amber-midnight
Original Tagcast theme by Cyberdelia, under the repository MIT licence reproduced
below. Near-black content, plum navigation and soft gold accents; warm pale Light.

Nord · Tagcast
https://www.nordtheme.com/docs/colors-and-palettes/
Polar Night neutrals are used for Light-mode links and buttons so small text stays readable; Frost remains the brand and player accent. Dark links use a slightly lighter Frost derivative (#9bcbd8) to stay readable on selection tint; separators use a neutral derivative.

Solarized · Tagcast
https://ethanschoonover.com/solarized/
Light blue links/buttons use a darker derivative (#1c6a9f) for small-text contrast. Dark links use a brighter cyan derivative (#38b4ab). Secondary text uses stronger neutrals (#52676d in Light) than low-contrast syntax comments; separators and hover colours are small derivatives.

Gruvbox · Tagcast
https://github.com/morhetz/gruvbox
Secondary text uses stronger palette neutrals. The Light button hover (#923203) is a darker orange derivative; dark hover (#fea04b) is a lighter derivative. Light links use a darker orange derivative (#9d3503) on selection tint. Selection uses warm neutral surfaces rather than orange text backgrounds.

Catppuccin · Tagcast
https://catppuccin.com/palette/
Latte uses Subtext 1 rather than weaker secondary colours. Light selection is a pale mauve tint (#e5ddf1), with dark active navigation #313244. Links and hover colours use small mauve derivatives (#7330cb in Light; #d5b8f9 in Dark) for contrast on selected rows.

Rosé Pine · Tagcast
https://rosepinetheme.com/palette/
Dawn secondary text (#68637e) is a darker derivative of Subtle. Small Dawn links use Pine; rose remains a decorative accent. Dark secondary text uses brighter derivatives of Subtle (#aaa5c2), rather than low-contrast Muted; separators, hover and shell secondary colours are adjusted. Focus uses Iris.


Navidrome-inspired themes · Tagcast
https://github.com/navidrome/navidrome
Tagcast colour-only adaptations of palettes used by Navidrome. Spotify-ish,
Cave and Dracula use their Navidrome Dark palettes as a starting point, with
new Light variants. Tokyo Night pairs the Navidrome Light and Dark palettes.
Moonbase pairs Moonbase Alpha and Bravo. These downloads use Tagcast colour
roles only. No Navidrome JavaScript, stylesheets or assets are included.
Preview images show the Tagcast 1.9.7 demo library.

Spotify-ish: https://github.com/navidrome/navidrome/blob/90ae2b6640b04b4d36d9a30d91f5687f953bd3cc/ui/src/themes/spotify.js
Cave: https://github.com/navidrome/navidrome/blob/90ae2b6640b04b4d36d9a30d91f5687f953bd3cc/ui/src/themes/cave.js
Dracula: https://github.com/navidrome/navidrome/blob/90ae2b6640b04b4d36d9a30d91f5687f953bd3cc/ui/src/themes/dracula.js
Tokyo Night Light: https://github.com/navidrome/navidrome/blob/90ae2b6640b04b4d36d9a30d91f5687f953bd3cc/ui/src/themes/tokyoNightLight.js
Tokyo Night Dark: https://github.com/navidrome/navidrome/blob/90ae2b6640b04b4d36d9a30d91f5687f953bd3cc/ui/src/themes/tokyoNight.js
Moonbase Alpha: https://github.com/navidrome/navidrome/blob/90ae2b6640b04b4d36d9a30d91f5687f953bd3cc/ui/src/themes/moonbaseAlpha.js
