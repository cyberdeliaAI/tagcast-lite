# Changelog

## 0.3.10

- **Mini player.** Click the mini player icon beside the queue to keep the cover, track title and playback controls in a compact window. Press Esc to return to the same library page while the music continues.
- **Queue scrolling.** Scroll ahead to browse the queue without losing your place at the next track change. Choose **Jump to current** to follow playback again. The **Up next** panel has its own follow control.
- **Playlist covers and descriptions.** Open a playlist to see its iBroadcast description beneath the title and its custom cover beside it. Longer descriptions wrap. If the cover is unavailable, Lite shows up to four different album covers in track order. Playlist artwork loads when you open the playlist.
- **Album layout.** Album details start level with the top of the cover. On shorter album headings the playback buttons line up with the lower edge. Long titles and additional genres have room to wrap above the track list.
- **Lighter artwork loading.** Lite requests images sized for the view and reuses covers already in memory. Opening an album can now reuse the image from the album grid.
- **Theme colours.** The vinyl logo now follows your palette in the sidebar, sign-in screen and **About**. The theme editor previews the logo as you change colours.
- **In-app updates.** Starting with this version, **Settings → About → Updates** can check for a release and download it while music plays. Choose **Install and restart** when you are ready. Homebrew installations use the same cask for updates.
